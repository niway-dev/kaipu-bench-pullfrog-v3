## Context

ADR 0024 made a capture the preferences already handle end quietly, in a toast. The production
audit of desktop 0.14.0 (2026-10-20) found Capture showing the toast where the owner expected the
editor; the fix saved every capture and sent Capture to the editor, leaving the toast to Copy.

Using 0.16.0, the owner asked for the opposite (2026-11-02): the editor coming forward after
every Capture is the disruptive part, and a bigger toast, with the image as the card and its
controls on it, is better than showing the window. The decision is the same question 0024 and
the fix already answered twice, so it is written down to stop a third flip by accident.

## Decision

1. `decideAfterCapture` returns `open: "quiet"` for **Capture and Copy**. Copying still follows the
   action (Copy always) and the preference (Capture only when copy is automatic).
2. The quiet path ends in the **capture preview card**, drawn in the existing capture toast window:
   the screenshot fills the card, Saved and Copied are visible on it at rest, and on hover it shows
   Edit, Close and the states as buttons (Saved reveals the file, Copy copies).
3. The editor opens only from **Edit** or a click on the image, and that is the only path that
   brings the main window forward.
4. A shot that **landed nowhere** (vault write and copy both failed) still opens the editor, which
   holds the bytes and can retry.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep Capture → editor, enlarge only the Copy toast.** The smallest change, but Capture and Copy
  would differ only in the clipboard, and the owner's complaint is the window coming forward.
- **An "After capture: editor / preview" preference.** Covers both habits, but 0024 already
  rejected an after-capture preference, and the editor is one click away on the card. If the owner
  misses the old flow, this is the first thing to add, and it needs no change to the card.
- **A second floating window for the preview, the toast kept for errors.** Two windows with the same
  lifecycle rules to keep in sync, for no state the card cannot show.

## Consequences

- Capture no longer stamps the item as opened, so a fresh Capture appears on the Editor home's
  Continue list only after Edit (as Copy already did).
- The toast window's size depends on the shot's aspect ratio, and its thumbnail grows from 160 to
  532 px; the payload over IPC grows accordingly.
- The card gains Copy and Reveal actions in main; Open in Library leaves it.
- The capture-flow-fixes backlog page and decision 4 of 0024 describe behaviour that no longer
  holds once this ships; both point here.

## Non-goals

- Where the main window sits in the stack after a quiet capture (it is hidden for the shot and
  shown again inactive). Tracked separately if the owner sees it.
- Dragging the card into another app, shortcuts on the card, stacking cards.
- The recording-ready card.

## Reopen when

- The owner, or analytics once they exist, shows that most cards end in Edit (more than half of
  cards three months after shipping): the editor is the common path, and the preference above
  becomes worth its cost.
- The OS gains a system preview the app can hand off to (as macOS does for `screencapture` without
  `-x`) that offers Edit into Kaipu.
